About Neuroπ

A comprehensive platform for EEG research, focusing on speech and motor activity patterns in the brain

Project Overview

Neuroπ is a comprehensive platform designed for EEG research, focusing on speech and motor activity patterns in the brain.

This application enables researchers and practitioners to collect, process, analyze, and visualize electroencephalography (EEG) data. It provides a complete workflow from data collection to analysis, with specialized tools for studying the neural correlates of speech and motor activity.

By combining EEG recordings with synchronized audio capture and timestamping capabilities, Neuroπ allows for precise alignment of brain activity with speech or motor events, opening new avenues for research in neurolinguistics, brain-computer interfaces, and cognitive neuroscience.

The platform integrates data collection, signal processing, visualization, and neural network analysis in a unified environment, making it easier for researchers to explore the relationship between brain activity and speech or motor functions.

Trial Stages

Neuroπ's structured approach includes five distinct trial stages that allow for comprehensive analysis:

Stage 1

Vocal

Say the word out loud
Stage 2

Non-Vocal

Think about saying the word
Stage 3

Motor

Perform the action without speaking
Stage 4

Motor + Vocal

Perform the action while saying the word
Stage 5

Motor + Non-Vocal

Perform the action while thinking about the word

Data Processing Pipeline

1. Data Collection

Record EEG data synchronized with audio (when applicable) across the five trial stages. Mark timestamps for important events during the recording sessions.

2. Signal Processing

Process raw EEG data to identify speech/motor events and align them with audio or timestamps. Generate combined datasets ready for cleaning and analysis.

3. Signal Cleaning

Apply bandpass filters, notch filters, and Independent Component Analysis (ICA) to remove artifacts and improve signal quality. Generate train/test splits for machine learning.

4. Visualization

Visualize the EEG data with interactive plots showing different channels, stages, and events. Compare patterns between vocal and non-vocal trials.

5. Neural Network

Train recurrent neural networks (RNNs) on the processed data to recognize speech patterns from EEG signals. Use trained models for real-time prediction.

Hardware Compatibility

Neuroπ is designed to work with the Emotiv EPOC+ EEG headset, which provides 14 channels of EEG data at a sampling rate of 128 Hz.

Key hardware features:

  • 14 channel EEG recording
  • 128 Hz sampling rate
  • Wireless data transmission
  • Built-in battery for portable operation
  • Sensor locations based on the 10-20 system

The platform can also be extended to work with other EEG devices by modifying the data collection module.

About the Project

NeuroPi was developed as an open-source project to advance research in brain-computer interfaces and speech detection from EEG signals. The platform aims to make EEG research more accessible by providing an integrated environment for data collection, processing, and analysis.

The project is continuously evolving, with ongoing development focused on improving signal processing algorithms, expanding neural network capabilities, and enhancing the user interface. Contributions from the research community are welcome!
