About P300 Classification

The P300 component is a positive deflection in the EEG signal that occurs approximately 300ms after a rare or significant stimulus. It's widely used in brain-computer interfaces for:

Applications
  • •Speller systems
  • •Menu navigation
  • •Yes/No communication
  • •Attention monitoring
Technical Details
  • Latency: ~300ms post-stimulus
  • Amplitude: 5-20 μV
  • Location: Parietal electrodes (Pz, P3, P4)
  • Paradigm: Oddball task
