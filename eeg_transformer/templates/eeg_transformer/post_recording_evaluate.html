How to Use
  1. Connect your EEG headset and ensure it's properly positioned
  2. Select a model trained on speech-related EEG data
  3. Set the recording duration (5-10 seconds recommended)
  4. Optional: Enter the target word you plan to speak
  5. Click "Start Recording & Evaluation" to begin
  6. During recording: Speak the word clearly 2-3 times
  7. Wait for the model to evaluate your EEG data
  8. View the prediction results
Tip: For best results, try to maintain consistent speaking patterns similar to how you recorded training data. Minimize head movements during recording.
