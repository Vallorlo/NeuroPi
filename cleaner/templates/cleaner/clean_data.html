Speech Detection from EEG: Quick Guide

Recommended Settings
  • Frequency Range: 4-50 Hz (bandpass) to capture theta, alpha, beta, and low gamma activity
  • Notch Filter: 50 Hz (Europe/Asia) or 60 Hz (US) to remove power line interference
  • ICA Method: Infomax works best for speech-related processing
  • Regions of Interest: Focus on frontal (F3, F4, F7, F8) and temporal (T7, T8) regions
Speech-Related EEG Bands
  • Theta (4-8 Hz): Language processing and working memory
  • Alpha (8-13 Hz): Speech inhibition and attention
  • Beta (13-30 Hz): Motor planning for speech production
  • Gamma (30-50 Hz): Higher linguistic processing and integration
