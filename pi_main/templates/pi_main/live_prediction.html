Live Prediction Instructions
How to use the Live Prediction tool:
  1. Select a trained model from the dropdown
  2. Choose a participant (optional)
  3. Set the sample duration (default: 5 seconds)
  4. Enter a target word if you're testing specific words
  5. Click "Start Prediction"
  6. Think about or speak the word during the recording period
  7. View results in real-time
Tips for best results:
  • Ensure the EEG headset is positioned correctly and has good contact
  • Try to minimize movement and muscle tension
  • Focus clearly on the word you're thinking about
  • For vocal tests, speak clearly but naturally
  • Try multiple attempts to improve accuracy
Troubleshooting:
  • If the EEG status indicator remains red, check your headset connection
  • If predictions seem random, try increasing the sample duration
  • Different models may perform better with different participants
