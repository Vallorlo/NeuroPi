    Participant Workflow Guide
    Getting Started
    1. Complete Your Profile
      • Add demographic information
      • Specify health conditions
      • Set participation preferences
    2. Set Your Availability
      • Choose available days and times
      • Set session limits
      • Add special requirements
    3. Join Research Studies
      • Create participation requests
      • Choose specific researchers or open invitations
      • Wait for researcher approval
    Research Process
    1. Consent Forms
      • Review assigned consent forms
      • Ask questions if unclear
      • Sign digitally when ready
    2. Session Participation
      • Attend scheduled EEG sessions
      • Follow researcher instructions
      • Provide feedback after sessions
    3. Ongoing Management
      • Update availability as needed
      • Withdraw consent if desired
      • View your participation history
    Important Notes
    • Participation is completely voluntary
    • You can withdraw from studies at any time
    • All data is kept confidential and secure
    • You have the right to ask questions about any procedure

    Researcher Workflow Guide
    Setup & Preparation
    1. Complete Researcher Profile
      • Add institution information
      • Specify research areas
      • Set contact details
    2. Create Consent Forms
      • Design study-specific consent forms
      • Include all required information
      • Set expiration periods
    3. Review Participation Requests
      • Check participant eligibility
      • Approve suitable candidates
      • Provide feedback messages
    Participant Management
    1. Assign Consent Forms
      • Send forms to approved participants
      • Set due dates and priorities
      • Monitor signing status
    2. Schedule Sessions
      • Coordinate with participant availability
      • Set session details and location
      • Send reminders and confirmations
    3. Conduct Research
      • Follow approved protocols
      • Ensure participant comfort
      • Document session outcomes
    Compliance Requirements
    • Ensure all participants have valid consent before data collection
    • Respect participant withdrawal requests immediately
    • Maintain confidentiality of all participant data
    • Follow institutional review board (IRB) guidelines
