How the Moving Window System Works:
  • Left Motor Imagery: Moves highlight window LEFT within the current letter group (wraps around)
  • Right Motor Imagery: Moves highlight window RIGHT within the current letter group (wraps around)
  • Rest: Confirms and submits the letter under the current window (based on last prediction)
  • Feet: Inserts a space between words
  • 2-Second Cooldown: Prevents prediction spam - wait for "Ready" status
  • P300 Word Selection: When word suggestions appear, focus on the desired word to select it
  • Window Navigation: Each group has its own moving window that wraps around the edges

  • Ctrl+2: Simulate Right Hand prediction
  • Ctrl+3: Simulate Rest prediction
  • Ctrl+Space: Simulate Feet prediction
